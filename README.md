## Leonardo F. Nascimento

Computational Social Scientist and Associate Professor at the Federal University of Bahia (UFBA), Brazil. PhD in Sociology (UERJ / EHESS). My research focuses on digital methods, computational sociology, and the sociology of knowledge — applying NLP, network analysis, and machine learning to study social phenomena in the digital age.

I coordinate the [Digital Humanities Lab at UFBA (LABHDUFBA)](https://github.com/LABHDUFBA) and serve as Data Analyst at CADE (Brazilian antitrust authority).

### Research Interests

- Digital sociology & computational social science
- Disinformation ecosystems and far-right publics on Telegram
- AI agents and epistemology of LLMs in social research
- Health information integrity and public communication
- Public finance data and civic tech

### Active Projects

- **AI Agency and Infrastructures in Social Research** — Epistemology of LLMs, RAG systems, and autonomous agents in social science (2025–present)
- **Health Information Integrity** — Multi-platform disinformation and public health impacts (CNPq, 2024–present)
- **Digital Democracy** — Disinformation ecosystems on Telegram during Brazilian elections (CNPq / InternetLab, 2022–present)

### Recent Work
